# Business Problem

## Overview

International trade is one of the most important drivers of economic growth. Governments, economists, multinational corporations, investors, and supply chain managers rely on trade data to understand how countries perform in global markets, identify emerging opportunities, evaluate economic health, and make informed strategic decisions.

Although several organizations such as the **United Nations Comtrade Database** and the **World Bank** publish large volumes of trade and economic data, these datasets are distributed across different platforms, formats, and structures. As a result, performing meaningful analysis requires significant effort in collecting, cleaning, integrating, and transforming data before any business insights can be generated.

Organizations often face challenges such as:

* Trade data and economic indicators are stored in separate data sources.
* Large datasets require extensive preprocessing before analysis.
* Manual reporting is time-consuming and prone to errors.
* Decision-makers lack interactive dashboards for exploring trade performance.
* Identifying trends, growth opportunities, and country performance requires combining multiple datasets and performing complex calculations.

This project addresses these challenges by building a centralized analytics platform that integrates global trade data with macroeconomic indicators into a single analytical data warehouse.

---

# Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to design and develop an end-to-end Global Trade & Supply Chain Intelligence Platform capable of transforming raw international trade data into meaningful business insights.

The platform enables users to:

* Analyze export performance across countries and years.
* Compare global export rankings.
* Measure year-over-year export growth.
* Relate export performance with GDP, population, and economic growth.
* Identify countries with improving or declining trade performance.
* Perform scenario analysis using Microsoft Excel.
* Explore interactive dashboards using Power BI.
* Support data-driven decision-making through visual analytics.

---

# Business Questions

The platform is designed to answer important business questions such as:

* Which countries are the largest exporters in the world?
* How have exports changed over time for each country?
* Which countries are growing their exports the fastest?
* How does GDP influence export performance?
* Does higher population always result in higher exports?
* Which countries have the highest exports relative to GDP?
* Which economies are becoming more dependent on international trade?
* Which countries have experienced declining exports over recent years?
* How do economic indicators influence international trade?
* What would happen if exports, GDP, or population changed under different business scenarios?

# Scope of the Project

The project focuses on building a complete end-to-end analytics solution covering the entire data lifecycle:

* Data extraction from public APIs.
* Data cleaning and transformation using Python.
* Data storage in a MySQL analytical database.
* SQL-based data warehouse and analytical views.
* Microsoft Excel executive reporting and scenario analysis.
* Interactive Power BI dashboards.
* Version control and documentation using Git and GitHub.

The project uses publicly available international trade and economic datasets covering multiple countries and multiple years, making it suitable for demonstrating real-world data analytics, business intelligence, and reporting workflows.
